Azamd does not collect any personal data or information from its users. You can enjoy the app without creating an account or providing any personal details.

Since Azamd does not collect personal data, no data is shared with third parties. Your privacy is our top priority.

While Azamd does not store personal data, we implement appropriate security measures to protect the app and its users. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the updated date will be revised accordingly. We encourage users to review the policy periodically.
